About Par0dy

Par0dy is a collection of hand-drawn notebook creatures inspired by chaos, graffiti, random thoughts, and imperfect creativity. Every NFT feels like a page torn from a sketchbook filled with strange ideas, rough doodles, and expressive artwork. No two pages are alike, but they all belong to the same notebook.
